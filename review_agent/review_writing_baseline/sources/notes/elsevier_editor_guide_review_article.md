# Elsevier Researcher Academy: Editor’s guide to writing a review article — Notes

Source: https://researcheracademy.elsevier.com/writing-research/technical-writing-skills/editor-guide-writing-review-article  
Local policy: summary only

## Key takeaways (distilled)

- Clarify review type + audience; “what question does the review answer?” is more important than listing all papers.
- Define selection logic: even narrative reviews benefit from transparent inclusion logic and coverage claims.
- Focus on **critical synthesis**: compare strengths/weaknesses, explain why results differ, and identify gaps.
- Keep a consistent organization scheme (taxonomy, pipeline, task, architecture, etc.) and reuse it across sections and tables.

## Candidate rules

- R-SURVEY-SCOPE: Must explicitly state audience + coverage boundaries + what is out-of-scope.
- R-SURVEY-COVERAGE: Must state corpus acquisition approach (databases/years/keywords) and known blind spots.
- R-SURVEY-COMPARISON: Must include at least one “cross-paper comparison table” with consistent columns.
