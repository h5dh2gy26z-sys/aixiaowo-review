# Proceedings of the IEEE: preparing your paper — Notes

Source: https://proceedingsoftheieee.ieee.org/resources/preparing-your-regular-paper/  
Local policy: summary only

## Key takeaways (distilled)

- High-quality papers are defined by **clarity, insight, and critical evaluation**.
- A good overview must provide a coherent narrative and help readers make decisions (what to use, when, and why).
- Systematic checklists alone are insufficient if they do not produce genuine synthesis and actionable insight.

## Baseline implications

- Rubric should require **decision-support artifacts**:
  - “Which approach should I choose?” tables
  - “When does it fail?” limitations
  - “What is missing?” gaps and future directions
