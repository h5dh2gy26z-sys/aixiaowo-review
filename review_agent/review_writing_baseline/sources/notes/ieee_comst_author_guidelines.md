# IEEE COMST author guidelines (PDF) — Notes

Source: https://www.comsoc.org/media/811/download  
Local policy: summary only

## Key takeaways (distilled)

- Survey papers are expected to provide **taxonomy**, **tables**, and **insight**, not only summaries.
- References should be **recent and relevant**; reviews should reflect the current state near the time of submission.
- The paper should highlight open challenges and future research directions based on evidence.

## Candidate measurable rubric items

- M-SURVEY-TAXONOMY: `taxonomy.yaml` exists and all included papers map to a leaf category.
- M-SURVEY-TABLES: At least N comparison tables exist with stable schemas (methods / datasets / metrics / assumptions / compute / reproducibility).
- M-SURVEY-RECENCY: For years within the corpus, compute the share of refs in the last K years (K configurable per field).
- M-SURVEY-FUTURE: Each main section ends with a short “open problems” list.
